# Vivid Analytics

We build data products that help people understand complex information and make better decisions.

Our work brings together product analytics, data engineering and clear presentation.

## Tenderline.uk

We develop and operate [Tenderline.uk](https://tenderline.uk/), a UK procurement intelligence platform for bid teams and suppliers.

Tenderline.uk connects official procurement data with buyer and supplier history, competition context and contract expiry information.
