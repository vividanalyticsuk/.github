We build data products that help people understand complex information and make better decisions.

## Tenderline.uk

We develop and operate [Tenderline.uk](https://tenderline.uk/), a UK procurement intelligence platform for bid teams and suppliers.

Tenderline.uk connects official procurement data with buyer and supplier history, competition context and contract expiry information.

## Open Datasets & Research

We publish reproducible open datasets derived from official UK Find a Tender OCDS releases (Open Government Licence v3.0 / CC BY 4.0):

- [**UK Procurement Portals Ranking 2026**](https://github.com/vividanalyticsuk/uk-procurement-portals-2026) - Open 95-column CSV comparing Britain's 18 main e-tendering portals across 25,178 procedures (£53.37bn in awards) and 8,293 completed PA 2023 lots ([interactive study](https://tenderline.uk/research/uk-procurement-platforms-ranking-2026)).
- [**Single-Bid Contracts in UK Public Procurement (2024-2026)**](https://github.com/vividanalyticsuk/uk-single-bid-contracts-2024-2026) - Lot-level single-bid competition rates and stage attrition across UK sectors, regions, buyer types, and value bands ([interactive study](https://tenderline.uk/research/single-bid-contracts-2024-2026)).
- [**Awesome UK Public Procurement**](https://github.com/vividanalyticsuk/awesome-uk-public-procurement) - Curated directory of UK public-procurement portals, OCDS APIs, open-source tooling, entity registers, and open datasets.
